How it works

A Ring event becomes a short, private, spoken description.

RingWebhookbutton_press or motion_detected, HMAC-SHA256 verified
→
RingSnapshotimage download at the event timestamp
→
Strands agentDescribe + matchvision observations, caregiver's expected visitors
→
ResidentSpeak + chimescreen reader, speech, vibration, Chime tone
Guardrails are code, not prompts

Strands BeforeToolCallEvent hooks block any announcement that guesses gender, age, race, religion, health or a name, runs over 28 words, or claims an expected visitor the evidence doesn't support.

Why it matters

Knowing who's at the door is independence.

  • Answer the door with confidence, or don't
  • Packages get noticed before they're lost
  • Caregivers set expectations once, and step back
  • Nothing about a visitor's identity is ever guessed or stored
